2b2tmuseum.org has over 100 warps available for you to see. To browse them, type /warp to leave Survival mode and open the Warp GUI. Each warp has a relevant icon chosen for it. Hover over it to see some information about it, and click to teleport. Upon warping, a book will appear in your hotbar. You can open this book to see if there are any neat facts written about the particular warp you have visited.

If you'd like to return to survival mode, you can run /freecam or /fc. The server will kick you and restore your player data to what it was before you left survival mode.
