2b2tmuseum.org is a Survival semi-Vanilla Minecraft server running on Minecraft 1.21.4. 2b2tmuseum archives historic locations from 2b2t for all to see on a playable Survival anarchy world.

  • Viewing the historic locations is as easy as typing /warp. A GUI will appear listing every possible location that you can visit. Simply click a location to teleport.
  • To resume your vanilla Survival gameplay, you can run /freecam or /fc, and your player-file will be reverted to exactly how it was before you left Survival mode.
